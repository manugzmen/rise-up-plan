4 DAYS ZONE 2 + VO2 PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD
The Rules
read once, apply always
  • 01
    Progressive overload. Each week +1–2 reps; at the top of the range, move to a harder variation.
  • 02
    Leave 1–2 reps in reserve. Not to failure every set.
  • 03
    Zone 2 = can talk, can't sing (≈6–7 min/km). Weekly: ~15–18 km Z2 + 1 sprint day.
  • 04
    Warm up 5 min before lifting (elliptical or rotation discs).
  • 05
    Rest = growth. Off days: walk, hike or mobility.

3-day version: merge D2 + D3 into one push/legs session and keep D1 and D4. You still hit Zone 2, sprints and full-body strength.

4-Week Progression
how to level up without plateauing
WK 1Find your baseline reps, 1–2 in reserve.
WK 2+1–2 reps each set · +0.5–1 km on each Z2 run.
WK 3+1–2 reps again, or harder variation (feet elevated, 3s slow tempo, archer push-ups).
WK 4Hardest variations + 1–2 extra sprints. Too fatigued: deload, then restart stronger.

Path to more pull-ups

Negatives (jump up, lower 5s) → band-assisted → full reps → add reps weekly → weighted / archer.

✓ Prioritize · eat plenty

  • Protein every meal — eggs, chicken, lean meat, fish, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey.
  • Good carbs — rice, oats, potato, fruit, whole grains. Your fuel, don't fear them.
  • Healthy fats — olive oil, avocado, nuts, whole eggs.
  • Veggies every meal — fiber, micronutrients, fullness.
  • Water — 2.5–3 L/day, more on run days.

✕ Limit · little, not banned

  • Soda and sugary drinks (empty calories).
  • Fried / fast food and pastries / refined sugar.
  • Ultra-processed packaged snacks.
  • Alcohol — slows recovery and muscle growth.
The Plate Trick
